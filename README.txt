STORY ARCHIVE — HTML & CSS

Open index.html in a browser. No installation needed.

styles.css controls colors, spacing, and layout.
index.html is your home page.
story.html holds a story description and chapter list.
chapter-1.html and chapter-2.html hold chapter text.

To post a new story, duplicate story.html and the chapter files with unique filenames. Update the title, description, paragraphs, and navigation links. Add a story-row link on index.html. Upload all files together to your hosting service, or ask Sites to update your website with your writing. Editing these downloaded files does not automatically update your hosted website.

All current story content is placeholder text.
